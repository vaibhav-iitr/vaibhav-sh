Investment thesis

I invest in early-stage companies in fintech, marketplace, and AI where the core product challenge is earning and maintaining trust — at scale, under adversarial conditions, without sacrificing growth.

The companies I find most interesting are ones where integrity is structural. Where a failure of trust is not a policy problem or a PR event, but an existential one. That is the space I have operated in for fifteen years, and the space I understand from the inside.

What I bring beyond capital

Fifteen years at the intersection of product, data, and trust: founding a digital payments business that was acquired, leading financial products for 40M+ users, and running marketplace integrity at Booking.com at the scale where trust failures become company-level events.

If you are building in payment infrastructure, marketplace quality, AI-native risk, identity, or any domain where the core question is "how do you know this is real?" — I can be a thinking partner on the product and operating architecture behind it.

What I'm not looking for

Consumer social without a trust or integrity challenge at the core. Pre-revenue concepts without a specific problem to solve. Founders looking for a name on a cap table more than a thinking partner who will push back.
